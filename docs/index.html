Lucas Åkesson
Environmental Effects Post Processing Audio

My contribution to Shader Bender involved working on the environmental setting of the experience. This involved creating weather effects such as rain, lightning and the clouds in the sky, getting the right look using post-processing, and enhancing the experience with an ambient soundscape. All weather effects were created using Unity’s particle systems, and manipulating these to achieve the desired effect. The clouds are made using a Unity URP shader to achieve a fake volumetric-rendering effect. As such, the clouds are just a shader on a simple plane, with some added effects to make them feel more 3D. The post-processing was done using global volume, with effects such as bloom, vignette, and white balance. All of the sounds I included in the soundscape, such as rain and lightning, and the background track of the experience, were created using audio from FreeSound.

David Giraldo
Volume Rendering HLSL Wind FX

In the Shader Bender project, I contributed by researching and implementing the wind-bending power using volume rendering techniques. I used HLSL to design a dynamic, visually compelling procedural effect for the wind dome and optimized its interaction with the player environment. I ensured seamless communication between the CPU and GPU for real-time updates. This work deepened my expertise in shader programming, procedural noise generation, and volume rendering techniques.

William Rolf
Fire Element FX Fire Interaction Audio

For my contribution to the Shader Bender project, I focused primarily on the fire element and object destruction part of the interaction. My research led me to finding resources for both volumetric and particle-based fire systems, the latter of which was demonstrated in a Q&A session with developers of the game “Diablo III” and showed that layered particle systems yield far better performance. Therefore I set about creating shaders that utilize layered particle systems for all kinds of fire interactions that I set up, from calling down meteors to casting a flamethrower and burning the environment around the player in real-time using motion tracking data. This project taught me how to handle complex interactions combined with particle system VFX techniques.
